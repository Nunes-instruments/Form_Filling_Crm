NUNES V6.6.6 - WHATSAPP INSTANT OPEN
=====================================

Purpose
-------
Make the WhatsApp Web Login action feel immediate and reduce the time between
linking WhatsApp and automatic message sending.

Changes
-------
1. /api/whatsapp/prepare no longer waits for Chromium / WhatsApp page load.
2. A cold WhatsApp resident starts in the background and the login request is replayed automatically.
3. The sidecar marks the login as opening immediately when the owner presses the button.
4. The WhatsApp Chromium launch removes nonessential Chrome startup features.
5. The WhatsApp resident runs AboveNormal priority.
6. Settings polls active WhatsApp login at 350 ms instead of 900 ms.
7. Service-form pending WhatsApp sending polls at 650 ms instead of 2500 ms.
8. Existing linked WhatsApp session, Gmail, Gemini, Purchasing and Servicing data are preserved.
9. Staff PCs require no ZIP. After the main server update they only refresh/open NUNES Operations.

Versions
--------
Main platform: 6.6.6
Servicing: 1.1.37
WhatsApp sidecar: 3.3.0
