SERVICEFLOW V1.1.30 - EMAIL + WHATSAPP SETUP

EMAIL (GOOGLE OAUTH)
1. Enable Gmail API in Google Cloud.
2. Create a Desktop app OAuth client and download the JSON.
3. Open ServiceFlow -> Settings -> Google Gmail Connection.
4. Upload the OAuth client JSON.
5. Click Sign in with Google and select the sender Gmail account.
6. After Google returns to ServiceFlow, the connected Gmail address is shown.

No Gmail App Password and no SMTP password are used.

WHATSAPP
1. Open ServiceFlow -> Settings -> WhatsApp Web Login.
2. Scan the QR with WhatsApp -> Linked devices -> Link a device.
3. The status changes to Connected.
4. Save & Preview sends automatically when the customer number is available.

Email and WhatsApp are independent; failure or absence of one channel does not block saving or the other channel.
