NUNES V19 - OWNER OLD DATA CLEANUP OPTION
=========================================

New owner-only file:
  OWNER_REMOVE_OLD_DATA_BEFORE_DATE.bat

How it works:
- Enter the FIRST date you want to keep (YYYY-MM-DD).
- The tool previews how many Purchasing and Servicing records are older.
- Records on the selected date and after it are kept.
- Missing/unreadable-date records are kept for safety.
- Before any deletion, an automatic recovery backup is stored under:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\NUNES Operations\OwnerArchive\
- Only after typing the exact DELETE BEFORE <date> confirmation does cleanup run.
- Related Servicing attachment folders are backed up and removed from active storage.
- Existing sequence counters are preserved so cleanup does not intentionally reuse old service numbers.

NO UI, WhatsApp, Gmail, Servicing startup, Purchasing workflow, report design, or normal business logic was changed.
