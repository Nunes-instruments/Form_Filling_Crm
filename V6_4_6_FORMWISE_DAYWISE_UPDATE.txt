NUNES OPERATIONS WORKSPACE V6.4.6
FORM-WISE + DAY-WISE FORM FILLING UPDATE

WHAT WAS ADDED
1. Dashboard now shows total forms filled across both workflows.
2. Purchasing form count is shown separately.
3. Servicing form count is shown separately.
4. Forms filled today is shown separately.
5. This-month form counts are shown for Purchasing and Servicing.
6. Day-wise table shows the last 14 days with:
   Date | Purchasing Forms | Servicing Forms | Total Forms
7. Reports page also shows the same form-wise and day-wise summary.
8. Counts are calculated from EXISTING saved records only.
   Purchasing uses record created_at (legacy fallback: quote_date).
   Servicing uses createdAt (legacy fallback: jobDate).
9. No old record is rewritten and no duplicate record is created for analytics.
