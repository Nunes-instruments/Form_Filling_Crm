GEMINI FORM READER FIX - SERVICE OPERATIONS 1.1.35

- Old saved/default Gemini 2.5 Flash model could return: model is no longer available to new users.

Changes:
- Default Service Form reader model: gemini-3.6-flash.
- Old saved gemini-2.5-flash automatically migrates to gemini-3.6-flash.

- Existing Service data, Gmail, WhatsApp and branch settings are not erased.
