NUNES V17 - WhatsApp QR Startup Optimization

Speed-only changes:
- Preserves WhatsApp token/session data.
- Clears only disposable Chromium caches before WhatsApp launch.
- Prevents rapid Settings polling from repeatedly relaunching Chrome after a failure.
- Explicitly disables WPPConnect login waiting and device-sync waiting during QR preparation.
- Adds lightweight loading telemetry for diagnostics.
- No UI, form, report, database, Servicing startup or company history changes.
